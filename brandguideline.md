# Brand Guidelines: FlowTrack (Modern Minimalist)

**Konsep Visual:** 
Sleek, modern, dan mengurangi *cognitive load*. Desain mengadaptasi kartu-kartu dashboard modern yang bersih dan responsif (Mobile-First).

**Tipografi Utama:** 
- **Font Family:** Poppins (Google Fonts - CDN universal).
- **Bobot (Font Weight):** 
  - Angka Saldo Utama: Poppins Bold (`font-weight: 700`)
  - Nama Kategori & Deskripsi: Poppins Regular (`font-weight: 400`)
  - Label / Status Tag / Header: Poppins SemiBold (`600`) & Medium (`500`)

**Palet Warna:** 
- **Latar Utama (Main Background):** Off-White (`#F8F9FA`)
- **Surface / Card Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) dengan elevasi shadow minimalis (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`)
- **Teks Utama (Primary Text):** Deep Charcoal (`#1A202C`)
- **Teks Pendukung (Secondary Text):** Slate Gray (`#64748B`)
- **Aksen Positif (HEMAT / Sesuai Target):** Vibrant Emerald (`#10B981`)
- **Aksen Peringatan (OVERBUDGET):** Soft Crimson (`#EF4444`)
- **Aksen Border Halus:** Light Gray (`#E2E8F0`)
- **Aksen Brand / Primary:** Indigo Accent (`#4F46E5` / `#6366F1`)
